📈 Methodology & Important Notes

Martingale Strategy: Double the bet after each loss until you win, then reset to the initial bet. The strategy aims to recover all previous losses plus win a profit equal to the original stake.

Confidence Intervals: The 90% confidence intervals indicate that we can be 90% confident that the true average falls within the given range. These are calculated using the t-distribution for robust statistical analysis.

Time Calculation: Assumes 1 minute per roulette spin, converted to days (24-hour periods).

⚠️ Risk Warning: The Martingale system can lead to catastrophic losses due to exponential bet growth. Even with high success rates, the few failures can be financially devastating. This simulation has a safety limit of $1,000,000 to prevent infinite losses.
